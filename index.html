Who We Are

Independent. Youth-Led. Fearless.

Masina Organization is a non-partisan, community-centered organization founded by young activists, journalists, students, and human rights defenders who witnessed increasing restrictions on civic freedoms in Uganda.

We exist to empower communities to understand and defend their constitutional rights, while promoting peaceful civic participation and accountability at every level.

Our Core Programs

Five interconnected programs building a more free and just Uganda.

Freedom of Expression

Supporting journalists, bloggers, artists, and community voices facing censorship or intimidation through advocacy and digital rights training.

Learn more

Anti-Police Brutality

Documenting abuses, supporting victims, and advocating for accountability and humane policing practices across Uganda.

Learn more

Youth Civic Leadership

Training young leaders to become advocates for democracy, peace, and accountability through workshops and mentorship.

Learn more

Community Peace & Dialogue

Creating safe spaces for peaceful discussion of social challenges, promoting unity across political and social divides.

Mental Wellness & Support

Peer support groups, counseling referrals, and healing retreats for activists and victims experiencing trauma and burnout.
