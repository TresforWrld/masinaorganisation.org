Defending Human Rights Since 2023

Voices Should Never Be Silenced

Masina Organization is a youth-led human rights and civic engagement organization based in Uganda, founded in 2023 to defend freedom of expression, promote democratic participation, challenge political oppression, and protect communities affected by police brutality and abuse of power.

Who We Are

An Independent, Non-Partisan Organization Defending Civic Freedoms in Uganda

We believe every Ugandan deserves the right to speak freely, organize peacefully, question leadership without fear, and live with dignity regardless of political belief, social status, religion, gender, or background.

Through advocacy, civic education, legal awareness, community mobilization, youth empowerment, digital activism, and peaceful engagement, we stand with citizens whose voices are often ignored, threatened, or silenced.

What We Focus On

Our Core Focus Areas

We work across multiple areas to defend freedoms, empower communities, and build a more just and democratic Uganda.

Freedom of Expression

Protecting the right of citizens, journalists, and activists to speak freely without intimidation or fear.

Anti-Police Brutality

Documenting abuses, supporting victims, and advocating for accountability and humane policing practices.

Youth Empowerment

Equipping youth with civic education, leadership skills, and opportunities for democratic participation.

Civic Education

Educating communities about constitutional rights, legal protections, and peaceful civic organizing.

Digital Rights

Defending online freedom, digital security, and the right to access information without surveillance.

Mental Wellness

Providing peer support, counseling referrals, and healing spaces for activists and survivors of abuse.
