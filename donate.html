Support Our Work

Your generous contribution helps us defend freedom of expression, protect human rights, and empower communities across Uganda. Every donation, no matter the size, makes a real difference.

Why Your Donation Matters

Masina Organization relies on the generosity of individuals like you to fund our programs, workshops, and advocacy efforts. Your donation directly supports our mission to defend freedoms and empower communities across Uganda.

Civic Education Programs

Fund workshops and training sessions that teach citizens about their rights and how to engage in democratic processes effectively.

Legal Support for Victims

Provide legal assistance and referrals to individuals who have faced intimidation, arrest, or abuse for exercising their fundamental rights.

Community Outreach

Expand our reach to more districts and communities, ensuring that even the most marginalized voices are heard and supported.

Digital Rights Advocacy

Support our work protecting online freedoms and ensuring that digital spaces remain open and accessible for all Ugandans.

Safe Spaces for Dialogue

Help us create and maintain safe spaces where communities can come together to discuss issues, share experiences, and build solidarity.
