Common Questions

Donation FAQ

How do I donate?

Contact us via email at info@masinaorganization.org or use the phone number listed on our contact page to arrange a donation. We will provide all banking details and a receipt upon contribution.

Can I donate from outside Uganda?

Yes! We welcome international supporters. Please reach out to us directly at info@masinaorganization.org and we will coordinate international transfer arrangements.

Will I receive a donation receipt?

Yes. Every donor receives a formal acknowledgment and receipt confirming their contribution and how it will be used within our programs.

Can I designate my donation to a specific program?

Absolutely. You can direct your contribution to any of our five core programs — from youth civic leadership to mental wellness support. Just let us know when you reach out.

How are donations reported and tracked?

Masina Organization maintains full financial transparency. We produce periodic impact reports available to donors. You can request a financial overview at any time by contacting us directly.
