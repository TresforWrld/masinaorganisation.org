Five Programs.
One Movement.

Interconnected initiatives working together to build a more free, just, and empowered Uganda from the ground up.

01
Program 1

Freedom of Expression Program

This program supports journalists, bloggers, artists, students, and community voices facing censorship, intimidation, or restrictions on their right to express themselves freely and safely.

  • Community dialogues on expression rights
  • Media freedom campaigns across Uganda
  • Digital rights training for activists
  • Storytelling initiatives & youth expression forums
  • Citizen journalism workshops
02
Program 2

Anti-Police Brutality Initiative

This initiative raises awareness about unlawful arrests, excessive use of force, harassment, and abuse by security authorities — documenting violations and supporting victims toward justice.

  • Documentation of reported violations
  • Community awareness campaigns on rights
  • Victim referrals and support services
  • Legal awareness sessions in communities
  • Peaceful anti-violence campaigns
  • Survivor healing circles
03
Program 3

Youth Civic Leadership Program

Masina Organization trains young leaders to become advocates for democracy, peace, and accountability in their communities — building the next generation of civic champions.

  • Leadership workshops & debate forums
  • Mentorship programs with experienced advocates
  • Civic education & digital activism training
  • Public speaking development sessions
  • Youth volunteer engagement networks
04
Program 4

Community Peace & Dialogue Program

This program creates safe spaces where communities can discuss social challenges peacefully and promote unity and reconciliation despite political or social differences.

  • Inter-community dialogue forums
  • Peacebuilding workshops & conflict resolution training
  • Community mediation support
  • Women and youth focused forums
  • Cultural exchange events for unity
05
Program 5

Mental Wellness & Activist Support

Activists and victims of abuse often experience trauma, fear, and emotional exhaustion. This program provides the emotional and psychological support needed to sustain the movement.

  • Peer support groups for activists
  • Mental wellness workshops & awareness
  • Counseling referrals & professional support
  • Healing retreats for survivors
  • Burnout prevention sessions
Education as Liberation

Our Workshops

We believe education is one of the strongest tools against oppression. Our practical, interactive workshops empower communities with knowledge and leadership skills.

Freedom of Expression & Digital Rights

Understanding the right to speak freely and safely navigate digital spaces in Uganda's civic environment.

Civic Education & Democracy

Teaching citizens about governance, elections, civic participation, and constitutional rights.

Community Organizing & Peaceful Advocacy

Helping young leaders organize peacefully and effectively within their communities.

Digital Security for Activists

Training participants on online safety, privacy protection, and responsible digital activism.

Human Rights Awareness

Educating communities on fundamental human rights and protection mechanisms available in Uganda.

Mental Wellness for Activists

Helping activists manage stress, fear, trauma, and burnout — sustaining the movement long-term.

Leadership & Public Speaking

Building confidence and communication skills among youth and community advocates at all levels.

Citizen Journalism

Empowering communities to document, report, and share stories of injustice responsibly and safely.

Conflict Resolution & Mediation

Training community members in peaceful conflict resolution and dialogue facilitation techniques.

In the Field

Our Activities

Community Outreach

We regularly visit communities to educate citizens about their rights, responsibilities, and opportunities for civic participation in shaping Uganda's future.

Youth Forums

We organize youth forums where young people discuss governance, democracy, unemployment, social justice, corruption, and civic responsibility.

Digital Advocacy Campaigns

Through social media campaigns, online storytelling, and digital awareness initiatives, we engage young Ugandans in conversations about justice and accountability.

Public Awareness Campaigns

We conduct campaigns against police brutality, violence against activists, corruption, political intimidation, and abuse of authority.

Community Dialogues

Masina facilitates peaceful dialogue sessions between community members, youth leaders, civil society actors, and local stakeholders.

Peace Walks & Civic Events

We organize peaceful events that encourage unity, nonviolence, and democratic participation throughout communities across Uganda.

"Silence Cannot Build a Free Society."

Defending freedom of expression, human rights, and democratic participation across Uganda since 2023.
