Our Commitment

Safety and Nonviolence

Masina Organization is firmly committed to nonviolence, safety, and peaceful advocacy in all aspects of our work. We believe that lasting change comes through dialogue, education, and peaceful engagement — never through force or harm.

Masina Organization operates on the fundamental principle that all advocacy for human rights must be conducted peacefully and nonviolently. We categorically reject the use of violence, intimidation, or any form of harm as a means of achieving social or political change.

Our approach is rooted in the belief that sustainable transformation comes through informed civic engagement, constructive dialogue, and the power of truth. We are committed to creating safe spaces where all individuals can express their views without fear of retribution or harm.

Core Principles

Our Safety Principles

These principles guide every aspect of our work and define how we engage with communities, partners, and stakeholders.

01

Nonviolence First

We are committed to nonviolent advocacy in all circumstances. We believe that violence undermines the very rights and dignity we seek to protect. Our methods are rooted in peaceful engagement, dialogue, and constructive activism.

02

Community Safety

The safety and well-being of the communities we serve is our highest priority. We take every precaution to ensure that our programs and activities do not put participants at risk, and we have protocols in place to respond to safety concerns.

03

Safe Spaces

We create and maintain safe spaces for dialogue, healing, and community building. These spaces are designed to be inclusive, respectful, and free from harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.

04

Do No Harm

We follow the principle of "do no harm" in all our activities. Before implementing any program or campaign, we carefully assess potential risks and take steps to mitigate any negative impacts on the communities we serve.

05

Informed Consent

We ensure that all participants in our programs provide informed consent and fully understand the nature of the activities, potential risks, and their right to withdraw at any time without consequence.

06

Confidentiality

We respect the privacy and confidentiality of all individuals who interact with our organization. Personal information is protected, and we maintain strict protocols for handling sensitive data and communications.

Our Commitments

What We Commit To

These are the specific commitments we make to every person who interacts with our organization.

Peaceful Methods Only

We use only peaceful, nonviolent methods in all our advocacy, campaigns, and community engagement activities.

Participant Protection

We prioritize the physical, emotional, and digital safety of everyone who participates in our programs and activities.

Risk Assessment

We conduct thorough risk assessments before every activity and implement measures to mitigate identified risks.

Inclusive Environment

We foster an inclusive environment where all individuals feel welcome and respected regardless of their background.

Zero Tolerance for Harassment

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or intimidation within our organization and activities.

Accountability

We hold ourselves accountable to these commitments and welcome feedback from communities and stakeholders on our performance.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review and update our safety policies and practices to ensure they reflect the highest standards of care.

Transparent Reporting

We maintain transparent reporting mechanisms for any safety concerns and ensure swift, fair resolution of all reports.

"Nonviolence is not a sign of weakness — it is the most powerful force for lasting change. When we speak truth with courage and compassion, we build a foundation that no oppression can shake."
— Masina Organization Founding Principle

Report a Safety Concern

If you have experienced or witnessed any safety violation, harassment, or conduct that violates our nonviolence principles, please report it to us. All reports are treated with the utmost confidentiality and seriousness.
