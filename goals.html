Our Goals

Seven Goals for Justice and Freedom

Our strategic goals guide every aspect of our work, from defending free expression to supporting human rights defenders and promoting accountability.

Strategic Goals

What We Are Working Toward

1

Promote Freedom of Expression

To protect and strengthen the right of citizens, journalists, artists, students, and activists to speak freely without intimidation or fear.

2

Combat Police Brutality and Abuse of Power

To document abuses, support victims, raise awareness, and advocate for accountability and humane policing practices.

3

Empower Young People

To equip youth with civic education, leadership skills, digital advocacy tools, and opportunities for democratic participation.

4

Strengthen Civic Engagement

To encourage peaceful community participation in governance, policy discussions, elections, and social accountability.

5

Support Human Rights Defenders

To create safe spaces, mental wellness support systems, and solidarity networks for activists and community leaders.

6

Expand Public Awareness

To educate communities about constitutional rights, legal protections, and peaceful civic organizing.

7

Promote Justice and Accountability

To advocate for transparency, justice, and institutional reforms that protect human rights.
