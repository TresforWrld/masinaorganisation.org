Whether you're a student, journalist, artist, or community leader — your skills and passion can help build a more just Uganda.

How You Can Help

Volunteer Roles

Community Outreach

Engage communities directly, distribute information, and facilitate civic discussions.

Workshop Facilitator

Lead our educational workshops on rights, digital safety, and civic participation.

Campaign Support

Assist with our active campaigns — from organizing to digital promotion and outreach.

Content Creator

Create graphics, videos, social media content, and storytelling for our platforms.

Research & Documentation

Help document human rights cases and compile research to support advocacy efforts.

Legal Support

Lawyers and law students can assist with legal awareness sessions and victim support.

Journalism & Media

Journalists and writers can help tell stories and maintain our news and blog section.

Event Organizing

Plan and execute community dialogues, peace walks, workshops, and civic events.
