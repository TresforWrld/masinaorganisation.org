Districts Covered

Reached thousands of young people through civic education programs across multiple districts in Uganda.

Organized community workshops across multiple districts, empowering citizens with knowledge about their rights and how to defend them.

Trained youth leaders in advocacy, digital rights, and peaceful civic engagement strategies.

Supported victims of intimidation and abuse through referrals and counseling support networks.

Built partnerships with grassroots organizations and community groups to strengthen the movement for justice.

Conducted peaceful awareness campaigns on human rights, accountability, and democratic participation.

Created safe spaces for dialogue, healing, and community building among activists and survivors.

Although challenges remain, our growing network of volunteers and community partners continues to strengthen the movement for freedom, dignity, and justice across Uganda.
