Our Activities

Action on the Ground

From community outreach to digital advocacy, our activities bring real change to communities across Uganda — educating, empowering, and building solidarity.

What We Do

Our Activities

Every activity we undertake is designed to educate, empower, and mobilize communities for peaceful change.

Community Outreach

We regularly visit communities to educate citizens about their rights, responsibilities, and opportunities for civic participation.

EducationRightsAwareness

Human Rights Workshops

We conduct workshops on constitutional rights, freedom of speech, peaceful assembly, digital security, community organizing, and human rights protection.

TrainingLegal RightsSecurity

Youth Forums

We organize forums where young people discuss governance, democracy, unemployment, social justice, corruption, and civic responsibility.

YouthDemocracyDialogue

Digital Advocacy Campaigns

Through social media campaigns, online storytelling, and digital awareness initiatives, we engage young people in conversations about justice and accountability.

DigitalSocial MediaAdvocacy

Public Awareness Campaigns

We conduct campaigns against police brutality, violence against activists, corruption, political intimidation, and abuse of authority.

CampaignsAwarenessAccountability

Community Dialogues

We facilitate peaceful dialogue sessions between community members, youth leaders, civil society actors, and local stakeholders.

DialoguePeaceCommunity

Peace Walks and Civic Events

We organize peaceful events that encourage unity, nonviolence, and democratic participation across communities.

PeaceUnityEvents
