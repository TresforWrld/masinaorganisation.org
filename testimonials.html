Community Spotlight
"I was a quiet student who believed nothing could change. Then Masina came to our university and showed us that peaceful action, informed advocacy, and unity can move mountains. Today, I lead a campus human rights club with over 200 members. We organize debates, screen documentaries, and mentor younger students about their civic responsibilities. Masina didn't just educate me — they ignited a fire that I now pass on to others. The change we seek begins with each one of us deciding to no longer be silent."

Peter K.

Student Leader, Makerere University
